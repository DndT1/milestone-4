Overview

This page uses real public FRED data from the last five years and builds an interactive view of how AI, robotics, and automation pressure may relate to unemployment and labor-market displacement. The chart expands the monthly payroll and unemployment records into thousands of observation rows across sector and occupation risk groups.

Social media productivity data

Social media can serve both as a communication channel and a distraction. A simple productivity view reports the following sample indicators:

Metric Value Interpretation
Daily social media use 42 min Moderate use may support learning and community, but time intensity matters.
Group attention loss 15% Users often lose focus after a short scroll session.
Task completion impact 18% Work sessions with frequent social-media check-ins may finish more slowly.
Productive platform use 67% When used for audience research, learning, and professional networking, social media can support job productivity.

Productivity improves when social-media use is planned, limited, and tied to a clear work goal rather than treated as a constant background channel.
